Multiple Nodules Rule (ACR TI-RADS)

When multiple nodules are present, no more than 4 nodules with the highest TI-RADS scores should be classified/followed, and FNA is not recommended for more than 2 nodules.

Sub-5mm Nodules & Significant Growth

Nodules < 5 mm (0.5 cm) require no follow-up even if classified as TR-5. Significant growth is defined as ≥ 20% increase in at least two nodule dimensions (minimum 2 mm) or ≥ 50% increase in volume.
